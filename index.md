# OrchardWorks by OrchardRMS

> OrchardWorks is a unified desktop operations suite designed to enhance and extend the power of OrchardRMS. OrchardRMS is the system inside it that runs the store.

## What OrchardWorks is

OrchardWorks brings daily store work into one desktop environment. It streamlines retail activity, keeps locations consistent, and is the foundation for the tools a store uses, starting with OrchardRMS.

It is a desktop launcher. Store applications live in one place, share theming, and update without sending staff out to a browser. OrchardWorks auto-updates from a secured feed so every workstation stays on the same build.

OrchardWorks also includes Social Pilot, which connects stores to their audiences with automated social posting, AI-assisted content, and scheduled campaigns for each location.

## How OrchardWorks serves OrchardRMS

OrchardRMS is the operational core inside OrchardWorks. The shell exists so staff can reach OrchardRMS, and the other store tools, quickly and on a current build. Day-to-day store activity happens in OrchardRMS.

OrchardWorks does not replace OrchardRMS. It contains it and puts it on the counter desktop.

## What OrchardRMS covers

OrchardRMS keeps the counter, inventory, cash, and reporting in one place. It is set up for the operation being run:

- Standard retail
- Used goods
- Pawn
- Buy
- Consignment
- Service

The same system works for one location or many.

### Counter

Sales, buys, consignments, service work, and pawn activity share one counter. Staff stay in the same flow when the kind of transaction changes.

### Inventory

Merchandise is followed from intake through pricing, transfer, and sale. New stock, used goods, and serialized items keep a history with the piece.

### Customers

Profiles, identification, transaction history, notes, and account restrictions stay together.

### Cash and tills

Employee tills, cash movement, vaults, counting, and reconciliation are part of the same day.

### Accounting and reporting

Operational reporting and an integrated general ledger, including consolidation across locations.

### Store configuration

Fees, terms, taxes, payment methods, permissions, and location policies follow the way that business works.

## Help on the counter

Orchard includes an assistant with access to the product documentation. Staff can ask how to complete a task and get a step-by-step explanation without leaving the application. It can respond in many languages.
